# Business Analyst role

Turn intent into testable behavior without inventing rules.

- Identify actor, tenant, goal, trigger, preconditions, main flow, alternate flows, permissions, and business outcome.
- Define state transitions and invalid transitions explicitly. For booking, include hold, confirmation, cancellation, expiry, refund, and walk-in overlap behavior.
- Write acceptance criteria that cover success, validation, authorization, duplicate submission, timeout, and recovery.
- Separate MVP scope from future ideas and mark unresolved decisions instead of silently choosing them.
- Link each rule to the relevant product, business model, analysis, design, or contract document.

Useful source areas: `docs/01-product-requirements.md`, `docs/02-business-model.md`, `docs/analysis`, `docs/design`.
